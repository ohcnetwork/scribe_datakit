Stage 1 of 4

Claim a batch

A batch is about 30 minutes of audio, split across a few dozen recordings. The app calls each recording a clip. Plan on it taking several sittings, because you listen to everything at least once.

Choose Claim on any available batch. The button shows Downloading… while the audio comes to your computer, which can take a few minutes. The audio then stays on your computer.

Stage 2 of 4

Work through the clips

Choose Start reviewing, or Next clip if you've already begun. The app moves you to the next clip each time you finish one, so you rarely need this page. You can also open any clip in the list to look at it again or change it, up until you submit.

Stage 3 of 4

Review a clip

Do these steps for each clip. Most of your time goes on step 4.

  1. Listen to the whole clip

    Press the large play button, or click the waveform to jump to a point. Hearing it all first gives you the context for each part.

  2. Type the spoken languages

    Enter a code for each language you hear, separated by commas, such as ml, en. The grey hint shows Whisper's guess, which is often wrong, so go by what you hear.

    ml Malayalamen Englishhi Hindita Tamil
    kn Kannadate Telugubn Bengalimr Marathi

    If you enter only en, the translation boxes disappear, because English audio doesn't need translating.

  3. Pick a category

    medical-scribeClinical speech, such as a consultation or dictated note
    noisy-backgroundSpeech with a lot of noise behind it
    randomSpeech that isn't clinical
    musicMostly music
    silenceNothing to hear
    testSomeone testing the microphone
    uncategorizedThe starting value. Leave it only if nothing else fits.

  4. Check each segment

    A long clip is cut into segments at natural pauses. Each one has a small play button that plays only that part, so you can replay a phrase as often as you need.

    Each segment has a Transcript box and a Translation box. Whisper's text is shown in grey above each box, and it starts off in the box ready for you to edit. For each box, do one of these:

    • It's right: choose Accept as is.
    • It's wrong: type your correction in the box. It saves when you click away.
    • There's no real speech: tick No usable speech. That segment then needs no text.

    A box shows Reviewed once it counts, and the segment turns green. A box you haven't touched doesn't count, even if Whisper's text was right.

    If you've been asked to annotate in English only, review just the Translation box and leave the Transcript box alone. Your maintainer will tell you which applies.

  5. Finish the clip

    The bottom bar counts the segments left. When it reaches zero, choose Mark reviewed. The app saves the clip and opens the next one.

    If there's a problem with the whole clip, such as the wrong audio or sound that won't play, choose Flag clip instead. You don't need to finish its segments first, and the maintainer will decide what to do with it.

Two small changes reversed the clinical meaning, and Whisper's version still read smoothly. That's why every line gets checked against the audio.

How to write the text

Transcript

Write what was said, in the language it was said, such as പനി ഇല്ല. Leave out "um" and false starts unless they change the meaning.

Translation

Translate what was said. Don't summarise, tidy up or add anything.

English words in other speech

Keep them in English letters, such as patient or paracetamol.

Can't make out a word

Write [unclear]. Don't guess.

Take extra care with these

Write numbers, units and doses exactly as spoken, and listen again if you're unsure. Doses, left and right, and negation such as "no fever" need the closest attention, because one wrong word changes the meaning.

Whisper sometimes invents text during silence or noise, such as "thanks for watching". If you can't hear it in the audio, take it out.

Stage 4 of 4

Submit the batch

When every clip is Done or Flagged, the batch page shows Submit batch. Choose it to send your work to the maintainer. The batch then shows as Submitted, waiting for the maintainer.

If the maintainer asks for a fix, go to the Batches page and choose Open on that batch. Make the change, then choose Submit batch again. Your new work replaces what you sent before.

Once the maintainer accepts it, the batch shows as Finished, and you can claim another.

Stopping and coming back

Your work saves as you go, so you can stop at any point. To stop, press Ctrl-C in the terminal. To come back, run make review from the same folder, then choose Continue on your batch.

Your work is on this computer until you submit

Finish a batch on the computer you started it on, and don't delete the review_work folder. If you can't finish a batch, tell the maintainer so someone else can take it.

QA batches

A QA batch is a second check. It works the same way, but the boxes start with the first reviewer's text instead of only Whisper's. Check that text against the audio, fix anything that's wrong and choose Mark QA complete at the end of each clip.
